The Story

"Started with $2,000. No investors. No corporate backing."

Pop's Exotic Sodas & Snacks was built from the ground up by Michael Minnis — a one-man operation fueled by hustle and a love for flavors you can't find anywhere else.

What started as a small idea became Mesa's go-to destination for rare international snacks, exotic sodas from 20+ countries, and the kind of treats that go viral on TikTok before they hit shelves anywhere else.

Whether you're chasing a childhood snack you thought was discontinued or hunting for the latest spicy challenge — Pop's has you covered.
